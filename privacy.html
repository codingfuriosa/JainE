Privacy Policy

How the Jain Group Customer Portal handles your information.

Who this covers

This policy applies to the Jain Group Customer Portal, the online account where a customer who has booked a flat with us can see their own booking, payments and construction updates. It is operated by Jain Group for its own customers. It is not a public service and has no general sign-up — an account exists only where we have created one for an existing customer.

What information we hold

The portal shows information that already exists in our records because of your booking. We do not ask you to supply it again. It includes:

  • Your name, email address, phone number and correspondence address
  • Your booking and unit details — project, tower, flat number, area, booking date
  • Your cost sheet, the demands raised against your booking, and the payments received
  • Construction photographs of your project, tower, floor and flat
  • Documents shared with you, and any support requests you raise through the portal

Where it comes from

This information originates in Farvision, the accounting and sales system Jain Group uses internally. Scheduled reports are exported from Farvision and loaded into the portal so that what you see matches our own records. We do not buy, collect or receive your information from any third party.

How we use it

Only to show you your own account and to run the portal — your financial position, your construction updates, your documents, and any support request you raise. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with third parties for their own purposes.

Our connection to Google

The portal's administrative system connects to a single Jain Group Google account in order to collect the scheduled Farvision reports that arrive there by email. That connection is deliberately narrow:

  • It looks only for messages from our own reporting address carrying a spreadsheet attachment
  • It downloads those attachments and marks the message as processed
  • It does not read other mail, and it does not send, forward or delete anything

This connection is to a Jain Group mailbox. It does not access any customer's Google account.

Who can see your information

  • You — signed in, you see only the units booked in your own name
  • Jain Group staff — authorised customer-care and accounts staff, to answer your queries and keep your records correct
  • Our service providers — the portal runs on hosted infrastructure (databases, file storage and email delivery). They hold the data on our behalf and act on our instructions only

Storage and security

Your information is held in access-controlled databases and file storage. Connections to the portal are encrypted in transit, each account can reach only its own records, and staff access is limited to those who need it.

How long we keep it

For as long as you hold a booking with us, and afterwards for as long as we are required to keep records of the transaction for legal, tax and accounting purposes.

Your choices

You may ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct anything that is wrong, or ask about deletion. Because most of this information forms part of our accounting records for your booking, there are limits on what can be deleted while the booking and its statutory retention period stand — we will tell you plainly what applies in your case.

Your login is personal to you. If you think someone else has access to your account, please contact us and we will reset it.

Contact us

For any question about this policy or your information, write to customercare@thejaingroup.com.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the portal handles your information, we will update this page and the date below.
