pauses between lines. When the recording comes back, every line is found in it automatically and
the video is re-timed so each scene starts on its line and each highlight lands on its word.

Does not repeat... Weekly... Fortnightly... Monthly... Quarterly... And Annually...

Fortnightly: one day, every other week...

Monthly: one date every month, or the last day...

Quarterly: one date, every third month...

Annually: pick the month, then the date. Here, the fifteenth of April...

Our task will repeat every Friday. Click Done, and save it...

A recurring task gets a purple border, so you can spot it in any list...

Now open the Recurring pill...

Due soon shows what needs doing next: anything overdue, then today, tomorrow, and the day after, with the deadline on each...

Change the view from this menu. Choose All frequencies...

The colours show where each one stands. Black: yet to come. Green: done on time. Red: done late. Bold red: not done, and overdue...

The number shows how many days early or late. For example... A plain Friday, with no number, is yet to come. Minus two, in green: done two days early. Plus two, in red: done two days late. Plus three, in bold red: not done, three days overdue...
