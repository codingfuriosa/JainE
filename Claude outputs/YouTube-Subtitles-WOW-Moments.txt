Introducing WOW Moments,
for the moments that win a customer over.

When a sales manager spends to delight a customer,
they now claim it back as a WOW Moment,
right inside Reimbursement.

The New WOW Moment button is only for our sales managers.
Nobody else sees it.

Click New WOW Moment.

Add the date, what you spent on, the amount,
and attach the receipt.

Your UPI details are already remembered from last time.

Submit, and your WOW Moment shows up outlined in purple,
so it's easy to spot.

Open it, and you can see exactly where it goes.

First, Shafat Mehar checks it,
and either forwards it to Accounts
or sends it back with a reason.

Then Accounts receives it and makes the payment.

Finally, it comes back to you.
When the money reaches you, click Done.

And remember, WOW Moments also count as a reimbursement.
So while one is open, a normal reimbursement cannot be raised.

You spend on the customer.
We will take care of the rest.
