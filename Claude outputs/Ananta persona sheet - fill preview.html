Notes

  • Age is worked out from the date of birth, as of 26 Oct 2026.
  • Flat Cost is the flat's own price before GST and parking. The full price including parking and GST is also available if you'd rather have that.
  • flat type is shown as the unit (block and flat number). If you meant something else by that column, it can be left blank.
  • Family Members Name: the father or husband named on the form (S/O, D/O, W/O) plus any co-applicant.
  • Not available from the Booking Form at all: Religion, Ad Seen Platform, Designation, Company, Office Address, Buyer Purpose, Investment, Budget, Other projects visited, Searching since, Children's ages, Site Visit On, Vehicle, Anniversary, Bank Loan, Bank Holder and UPI App.
