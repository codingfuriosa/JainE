1. What Sales have to fill

One page, four cards (Generate Form → NEW). Six boxes are required (red); the rest have a sensible default or can stay empty. Everything is pre-filled for the customer, and the customer can change any of it (nothing is locked any more).

What the customer fills afterwards

Step 1 Applicant detailsRelation (S/o, D/o, W/o, C/o) and father's / husband's name, date of birth (first applicant), occupation, PAN, Aadhaar; folded away: Spouse & anniversary, Passport & bank details (NRIs / foreign nationals). Joint applicants can be added (up to 6).
Step 2 Contact & addressResidential address, city, PIN; optional office address; where letters go and the permanent address (Residential / Office / Other).
Step 3 Occupation & sourceProfession, company, designation; optional "More about you" (income, community, newspaper, magazine, TV, radio); how they heard about us; up to 5 reasons for choosing the project.
Step 4 Unit detailsAlready filled by Sales - they can change block, floor, flat number, bedrooms, car park, area and price; payment plan; home loan Yes / No (and the bank).
Step 5 Booking amountPaid by (UPI / NEFT / RTGS, Cheque, Demand Draft, Card), amount, date, reference, bank (and branch for a cheque / DD); purpose of booking; booked through.
Step 6 KYC documentsPAN card and Aadhaar card photo or PDF for every Indian applicant (up to 2 files each); optional for a foreign applicant.
Step 7 Terms & signatureTick that the terms are read and agreed; the first applicant signs.
Step 8 Review & submitCheck everything (Edit on any section), then Submit.

What stays hidden until it is needed

Spouse & anniversary · Passport & bank details (opens by itself for a foreign applicant) · Office address · "Other" mailing / permanent address · "More about you" · the bank box (only if a home loan is wanted) · Bank & Branch (only for cheque / DD) · the second, third... applicant (only after "+ Add another applicant") · "Other" source. Empty answers are left out of the Review page and the Sales preview.

2. Sales / Administrator - Generate Form

1Where Sales starts

On the Booking Form Flow page (Accountability, flow 41) the "Generate Form" button sits next to "New Booking Form". Only people allowed to generate forms see it (Administrator only for now).

2NEW tab - an empty form

The page opens inside the ERP (menu and top bar stay). Four cards: Customer, Contact, Flat, Size & price. Everything Sales types here is pre-filled for the customer, who can still change it.

3NEW tab - Generate link with something missing

Each missing box turns red with its own message; the bar says how many things to fix. Nothing is created until it is right.

4NEW tab - filled in

Contact person follows the customer name unless typed over; the price shows commas and is written in words underneath. Three buttons: Cancel, Save draft, Generate link.

5Save draft

Not ready to send? Save draft keeps it privately for this Sales person (nobody else sees it) - it is listed under the DRAFTS tab and can be continued later.

6The link is ready

One link for this customer. Copy link, Open as the customer (to preview), or Generate another. The draft is removed once the link exists.

7DRAFTS tab

Saved drafts, newest first, with the date saved. Open continues it in the NEW tab; Delete removes it.

8Generated forms tab

Every link made, with its status: Not opened yet, Filling in (step n of 8, when saved), or Submitted with its reference number. Open links have Copy link and Edit; a Submitted one has Open form.

9Edit an open link

Edit reopens the details of a link that is not submitted yet - correct a name or price and save; the link stays the same. (If the customer has already started, their own saved answers are what they see.)

3. Customer - the form on a computer

1Step 1 - Applicant details, as the customer first opens the link

Title, name, nationality and date of birth are already filled by Sales (the customer can change them). Only one applicant is shown; Spouse and Passport / bank panels are folded away.

2Step 1 - what happens if something required is empty

A list "Still missing on this page" appears at the top, each item jumps to its box, and the box turns red. Nothing is lost.

3Step 1 - filled, with the two folded panels opened

Spouse & anniversary and Passport & bank details are hidden until the customer taps them (passport & bank open by themselves for a foreign applicant).

4Step 1 - a joint applicant added

"+ Add another applicant" adds a full block (up to 6). Joint applicants do not ask for date of birth or spouse. Each has a Remove button.

5Step 1 - a foreign / NRI applicant (third applicant)

Choosing another nationality makes PAN optional, flags the country with its flag, and opens the Passport & bank details panel as required.

6Step 2 - Contact & address, as opened

Mobile, email and contact person are pre-filled by Sales. The residential address is for the customer. The Office address is a switch that stays folded.

7Step 2 - with an office address and a different permanent address

Switching on "I want to add an office address" opens its fields and adds Office as a choice for letters. Choosing Other for mailing / permanent address opens a box for it.

8Step 3 - Occupation & source, as opened

Everything here is optional except "Other" (if chosen). "More about you" is folded away.

9Step 3 - "More about you" opened, "Other" source, several reasons

Choosing Other for "how did you hear" opens a required box. "Why did you choose this project?" takes up to 5 reasons, each can be removed.

10Step 4 - Unit details, as opened

Block, floor, flat number, bedrooms, car park, built-up area and total price are pre-filled by Sales ("As agreed with our sales team") - the customer can still change them. Home loan is No.

11Step 4 - Home loan "Yes" opens the bank box

The preferred bank box appears only when a loan is wanted.

12Step 5 - Booking amount, paid by UPI / NEFT / RTGS

Amount in figures and in words, date paid, UTR reference, bank. Purpose and "Booked through" below. (Sales person name and Lead ID are hidden for now.)

13Step 5 - paid by cheque

The labels change (Cheque number, Cheque date) and Bank (drawn on) becomes required with a Branch box.

14Step 6 - KYC documents, nothing attached yet

One card per applicant (named), a PAN box and an Aadhaar box. A foreign applicant's boxes are optional.

15Step 6 - photos attached (two photos for one Aadhaar)

Each box takes up to 2 files (photo or PDF) - e.g. Aadhaar front and back. Thumbnails show, with a × to remove. Files are saved the moment they are attached.

16Step 7 - Terms & signature, before reading

The three term lists are folded ("Open each section to read it in full"). The tick box and the signature box are both required.

17Step 7 - a term list opened

Tapping a heading opens the numbered clauses.

18Step 7 - ticked and signed

The first applicant signs in the box (mouse or finger). "Sign again" clears it. Only the first applicant signs.

19Step 8 - Review & submit

Everything the customer gave, section by section, with an Edit button for each (it returns straight to this page after the change). Empty items are hidden.

20Closing the page and opening the link again

The customer lands on the same step with every answer, attached file and signature still there (saved on our server as they typed).

21After Submit - thank-you screen

The customer gets a reference number. The form is sent to Sales ("Generated forms").

22The link opened again after submitting

Shown read-only, exactly as submitted. PAN and Aadhaar show only their last four characters; passport and bank say "Provided"; no document is shown.

23Sales / Administrator preview of a submitted form (signed in)

The same page, but for staff: every answer including full PAN / Aadhaar numbers, the documents as picture cards under each applicant's name, and the signature. Only what the customer gave - nothing else.

24Sales preview - a document opened large

Tap a card to see the picture large; the applicant's name is shown with it.

4. Customer - the form on a phone

1Phone - Step 1 as opened

Same form, one column. Sales' details are already in; the customer can change them.

2Phone - Step 1 with a joint applicant

Each applicant is a numbered block with its own Remove button; "+ Add another applicant" is at the bottom.

3Phone - Step 2 Contact & address

Mobile and email pre-filled by Sales; address typed by the customer; Office address is a switch that opens its own fields.

4Phone - Step 4 Unit details

Block, floor, flat, bedrooms, car park, area and price as agreed with Sales - changeable.

5Phone - Step 5 Booking amount

Amount, date, reference and bank; the amount is also written in words.

6Phone - Step 6 KYC documents

On a phone "Take a photo or attach a PDF" opens the camera. Up to 2 files per document.

7Phone - Step 7 Terms & signature

On a phone, tapping the signature box opens a large signing pad.

8Phone - the signing pad

A full-screen pad to sign with a finger. Done saves it into the signature box.
